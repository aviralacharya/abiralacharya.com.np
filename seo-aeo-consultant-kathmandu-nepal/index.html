The Search Landscape Has Shifted.
Is Your Brand AI-Ready?

Traditional SEO is no longer enough. Kathmandu is home to Nepal's most competitive business ecosystems. To stand out, you must satisfy standard search engine crawlers AND generative AI models.

As users migrate from classic queries to conversational prompts on platforms like ChatGPT and Google Gemini, your business must be structured, cited, and referenced in a way that machine-learning models trust and recommend.

Traditional SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

Focuses on indexing, keywords, crawlability, and links to rank your URLs on Google's search result pages.

Modern AEO / GEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Focuses on structured entities, schema, direct conversational answers, and semantic authority so AI engines cite your brand.

⚡ How Answer Engines Decide

AI models do not index the web like classic Googlebot. They extract and summarize answers based on specific authority signals:

  • Machine-readable JSON-LD Schema markup
  • Semantic entity linking & topical map clusters
  • High EEAT (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust) signals
  • Direct, conversational Q&A structured content
  • Mentions on trusted, authoritative directories

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is AEO and how does it relate to traditional SEO?
AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. While traditional SEO optimizes websites for search engines (like Google Search Pages) to get link clicks, AEO optimizes content to be directly parsed, understood, and recommended by AI answer engines and Large Language Models (like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews). AEO requires structured schema and semantic patterns.
Why does my Kathmandu business need localized search support?
Most local search intents (e.g., "digital agency Kathmandu" or "lawyer near me") trigger Google's Map pack. Localized SEO ensures your business details (Name, Address, Phone - NAP) are consistent across Nepal directories and optimized in Google Business Profile, placing you on the local map.
How long does it take to see rankings improve?
Technical fixes (like fixing robots.txt blockages or redirect loops) can show indexing improvements in a few days. Organic keyword rankings, domain authority, and AI model recommendation database updates usually compound over 3 to 6 months of systematic work.
Do you work on monthly retainers or one-time audits?
I offer both. One-time deep technical audits are great for developers who want a checklist of things to repair. For continuous growth, topic cluster expansion, and authority link acquisition, a monthly consulting retainer is recommended.
